## ⚠️ COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Too high pricing** - Start competitive, raise gradually
2. **Poor customer service** - Reply within 1 hour, always
3. **Ignoring reviews** - Respond to ALL reviews (good & bad)
4. **Not showing photos/videos** - People buy experiences they can visualize
5. **Complicated booking** - 3 clicks max to book
6. **No payment gateway** - Accept all methods (card, bank, ewallet)
7. **Seasonal thinking** - Book year-round (adjust pricing)
8. **Ignoring partnerships** - Hotels are your biggest customers
9. **No follow-up** - Email customers after trip, ask for reviews
10. **Underestimating operations** - You need a booking manager
