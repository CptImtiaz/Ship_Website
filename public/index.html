❓ FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions answered.

Do I need a fishing license?

No license needed for recreational fishing in Malaysian waters. Our boatman will provide all necessary equipment and know where it's legal to fish.

Can I bring my own food?

Yes! You can bring snacks and drinks. We also offer BBQ and food packages. No outside alcohol on safety grounds.

What's the weather policy?

Trips are cancelled only in severe weather. We'll reschedule with full refund or credit. Check forecast during booking.

Is it safe for non-swimmers?

Absolutely. Life jackets provided. Our boats are stable and our boatmen are trained in water safety.

Can I cancel my booking?

Yes. Full refund up to 48 hours before. 50% refund within 48 hours. No refund within 24 hours.

What time should I arrive?

Arrive 15 minutes early at the departure point. We'll provide exact location in your confirmation.
