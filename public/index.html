BEFORE YOU BOOK

Common questions.

Keep this section focused on your real policies, safety information and what guests should bring.

Is a boatman included?

Yes. Your private boat booking includes an assigned boatman for the trip.

Can beginners join a fishing trip?

Yes. Beginners can choose fishing equipment as an add-on and the boatman can help with the trip setup.

Can we bring our own food?

You can define your own food policy here. BBQ, drinks and other food packages can also be sold during checkout.

What happens if the weather is unsafe?

Use your official weather and rescheduling policy here. Safety should determine whether a trip can depart.

Are children allowed?

Family trips can be offered with appropriate life jackets and your own minimum-age or supervision requirements.

Can I book the whole boat privately?

Yes. The core SeaEscape experience is designed around private bookings for your own group.
